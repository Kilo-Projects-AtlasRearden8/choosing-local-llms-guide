Welcome

Welcome to Choosing and Running Local LLMs with Ollama. This guide is for anyone who wants to run an AI language model on their own computer — no account, no monthly fee, and nothing leaves your machine. It's written for beginners, step by step, in plain English.

Who this is for

  • You're curious about AI but don't want to send your data to a cloud service.
  • You have a computer and want to try running a local model.
  • You want privacy: your chats stay on your machine.
  • You're not a programmer — or you are and just want the simplest path.

You do not need to know how to code. You do not need a powerful gaming PC to get started. You just need a little time and this guide.

What you'll learn

  • What Ollama is and why it makes local AI simple
  • How to read model sizes (parameters and quantization) so you pick one that fits
  • What hardware you need (RAM, GPU, CPU) and how to match a model to your computer
  • How to install Ollama on Mac, Windows, and Linux
  • How to pull, run, chat with, stop, and remove models
  • Why nothing leaves your computer when you run a local model
  • How to use the printable model-selection worksheet to choose your first model

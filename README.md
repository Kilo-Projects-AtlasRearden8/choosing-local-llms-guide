# Choosing and Running Local LLMs with Ollama

A beginner-friendly manual for choosing and running large language models on your own computer with [Ollama](https://ollama.com). Nothing leaves your computer.

Written in plain English, step by step, like a friend would explain it. Published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October 2026) — beginner-friendly AI education, books, and practical tools.

## What's inside

This folder contains two editions of the same guide:

- `docs/` — Markdown manual, easiest to read and adapt
- `static/` — Self-contained HTML website you can open in any browser, including a printable model-selection worksheet

## Contents

1. [Welcome](docs/01-welcome.md) — who this is for and what you'll learn
2. [What is Ollama?](docs/02-what-is-ollama.md) — local AI in plain English
3. [Model sizes](docs/03-model-sizes.md) — parameters, quantization, and what the numbers mean
4. [Hardware needs](docs/04-hardware-needs.md) — RAM, GPU, and which models fit your computer
5. [Installing Ollama](docs/05-install-ollama.md) — Mac, Windows, and Linux, step by step
6. [Running your first model](docs/06-running-models.md) — pull, chat, stop, remove
7. [Privacy](docs/07-privacy.md) — why nothing leaves your computer
8. [Model-selection worksheet](docs/08-model-selection-worksheet.md) — fill it out and print it
9. [FAQ](docs/faq.md) — common questions

## The static site

Open `static/index.html` in your browser. Every page works offline, and the worksheet page is designed to print cleanly on one sheet of paper.
